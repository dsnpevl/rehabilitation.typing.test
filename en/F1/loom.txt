A loom is an amazing traditional machine used to weave beautiful cloth. It works by holding long vertical threads tight, which are called the warp. The weaver then passes horizontal threads, known as the weft, over and under them using a fast shuttle. This simple interlacing process creates strong fabric for nice clothes and blankets. From old hand-operated wooden frames to massive modern power machines, looms have shaped human clothing history for thousands of years.
